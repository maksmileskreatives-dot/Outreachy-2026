# Outreachy organizations research

## GNOME
GNOME develops free and open-source desktop software and applications. Designers contribute to user interface, user experience, icons, accessibility, and documentation.

## Wikimedia
Wikimedia supports projects such as Wikipedia and focuses on free knowledge for everyone. Designers work on accessibility, visual communication, documentation, and user experience.

## OpenStreetMap
OpenStreetMap is a collaborative mapping project used around the world. Designers contribute to mapping interfaces, visual design, documentation, and community resources.
